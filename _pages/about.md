I am a research scientist at the [Princeton Center for Information Technology Policy](https://citp.princeton.edu/), where I work with [Arvind Narayanan](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~arvindn/) and [Zeynep Tufekci](https://ai.sociology.princeton.edu/people/zeynep-tufekci) on multiple projects relating to [AI agent evaluation](https://sage.cs.princeton.edu/) and the [societal impacts of AI](https://ai.sociology.princeton.edu/). This past spring, I also worked with [Tim Hua](https://timhua.me/) as part of the [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) program on a project to study mechanisms for value reflection and systematization in frontier AI models.

## Current Projects

1. **Open-world evaluations:** I lead harness engineering, manage a team of six analysts, and direct reporting for [CRUX](https://cruxevals.com) (Collaborative Research for Updating AI eXpectations), a series of long-horizon, open-world evaluations of frontier AI agents on real-world tasks such as autonomous software development and autonomous AI research. Read the [position paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20520) introducing the project.
2. **AI agent reliability:** Alongside [Stephan Rabanser](https://rabanser.dev/) and the rest of the HAL team at Princeton, I am working to develop an index of AI agent reliability. Learn more at the [project website](https://hal.cs.princeton.edu/reliability/).
3. **Anthropomorphization and attachment:** With the [AI and Society Lab](https://ai.sociology.princeton.edu/) at Princeton, I am working to document the prevalence of anthropomorphization, attachment, and engagement with AI chatbots using a combination of synthetic benchmarks, big data analytics, and population surveys.
4. **LLM value reflection:** With Tim Hua, I am exploring how different LLMs iteratively refine their own constitutions, model specs, and system prompts.

## Background

I am a recent MPA graduate from Princeton, where I also completed a graduate certificate in statistics and machine learning. My broad research interests are in artificial intelligence, moral philosophy, intergenerational mobility, and social safety net implementation. I have also worked in civic technology, most recently at the US Census Bureau as a [Coding it Forward Data Science Fellow](https://www.codingitforward.com/), and prior to that, as a data analyst with the [Massachusetts Digital Service](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massachusetts-digital-service). I received my BA from Williams College in 2020, where I studied political economy, philosophy, and cognitive science. Outside of work, I am an avid runner and cyclist and love spending long days in the mountains.
